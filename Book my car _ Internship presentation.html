Renting a car shouldn't take five phone calls

Many rental agencies still take bookings by phone or social media. Customers can't see the fleet, compare prices, or know the total before they call. The agency tracks cars, bookings and customer requests by hand.

Project goals

  • Show the full fleet online, with photos, specs and daily price
  • Let anyone book a car in under a minute, with the total price calculated live
  • Give customers a space to follow and cancel their reservations
  • Give the agency one dashboard for cars, bookings, users and messages

Customer

Administrator

How authentication works

Stateless JWT sessions, with checks on both the server and the client.

1

Register

Mongoose validates the email and requires a password with upper case, lower case and a digit. A pre-save hook hashes it with bcrypt (salt 10).

2

Log in

The server compares the password with bcrypt and signs a JWT containing the user id and role, valid for one day.

3

Call the API

The client stores the token and sends it as Authorization: Bearer. verifyToken decodes it; isAdmin checks the role.

4

Guard the pages

ProtectedRoute redirects visitors to login and keeps customers out of the admin dashboard.

Role check

Roles are normalised to lower case on both sides, so Admin and admin resolve to the same permission.
